# Young Families (PLACEHOLDER – replace with the approved segment profile)

## Who they are

Couples aged 28 to 40 with at least one child under 10, mostly in Bangkok and big provincial
cities. Both parents often work. Budgets are tight and plans are long: school, a home, savings
for the children.

## Needs

- Protect the children's plans (school, home) if a parent falls seriously ill or can't work.
- Cover for hospital bills for the whole family.
- Save for the children's education step by step.
- Affordable plans that fit a monthly budget.

## Pain points

- Many expenses at once; insurance feels like one more bill.
- Little time to compare products or read policy details.
- Worry about what happens to the family if the main earner is out of work.

## Product fit

Health, critical illness and endowment (education savings) fit best.

## Channel habits

- Active on LINE and social media, often late in the evening after the children sleep.
- Respond to real family moments more than to product shots.

## Messaging do's and don'ts

Do:
- Show family moments: meals, play time, school mornings, weekends out.
- Keep the message about being ready for the family and keeping plans on track.

Don't:
- Show children in danger or parents in hospital.
- Use guilt or fear.
