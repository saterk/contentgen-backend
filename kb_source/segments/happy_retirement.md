# Happy Retirement (PLACEHOLDER – replace with the approved segment profile)

## Who they are

People aged 45 to 60 who are planning for retirement, and recent retirees up to about 65.
Many are salaried employees or business owners with grown or teenage children. Some also care
for elderly parents.

## Needs

- A steady income after they stop working, so they don't depend on their children.
- Cover for health costs, which rise with age.
- Keep their lifestyle: travel, hobbies, time with grandchildren.
- Simple, low-risk ways to grow and protect what they have saved.

## Pain points

- Worry that savings won't last long enough.
- Feel it may be too late to start planning.
- Find financial products complicated and are wary of hard selling.

## Product fit

Annuity and health fit best. Endowment suits those still 5–15 years from retirement.

## Channel habits

- Use LINE daily, mostly for family groups; read longer messages than younger segments.
- Trust advice from a known advisor or the bank branch.

## Messaging do's and don'ts

Do:
- Show active, happy retirement: travel, gardening, cooking, time with grandchildren.
- Talk about freedom, independence and peace of mind.
- Use calm, respectful language.

Don't:
- Show frailty, loneliness or hospital scenes.
- Talk down to them or call them old.
- Promise returns or use urgency to push a decision.
