# First Jobber (PLACEHOLDER – replace with the approved segment profile)

## Who they are

People aged 22 to 30 in their first few years of full-time work, mostly single. Many still live
with their parents or rent a room in the city, and some send money home.

## Needs

- Start saving and build good money habits early.
- Cover for hospital bills beyond what their employer provides.
- Look after their parents in the future.
- Small, flexible amounts that fit a starting salary.

## Pain points

- Think insurance is for older people.
- Salary feels too small to save after rent, transport and going out.
- Don't know where to start; find insurance language hard to read.

## Product fit

Health and endowment (starting to save) fit best. Critical illness is a later step.

## Channel habits

- Heavy social media and LINE users; prefer short, visual content on mobile.
- Respond to peers and real-life moments more than to formal brand messages.

## Messaging do's and don'ts

Do:
- Show everyday life: commuting, the first office, coffee with friends, a first trip paid by
  their own salary.
- Keep the language light, simple and positive. Show that starting small is fine.

Don't:
- Lecture or use fear.
- Use heavy financial jargon.
