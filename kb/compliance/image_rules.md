# Image rules (PLACEHOLDER)

- People are Thai (Southeast Asian appearance). Always say "Thai" when describing people in the
  subject and the image prompt.
- No text, numbers, logos or watermarks in the image. Copy is added in the layout.
- Avoid props that carry text: book titles, signs, screens, packaging, documents. If a book or
  phone appears, its cover or screen is plain.
- No hospital beds, IV drips, ambulances or other clinical scenes.
- No people in visible pain or distress.
- People look natural; no distorted hands or faces.
- Insurance-appropriate: calm, safe, everyday settings.
- Default output size 1080×1350 (4:5), subject in the middle third, space at top and bottom for copy.
