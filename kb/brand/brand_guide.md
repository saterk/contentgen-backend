# Brand guide (PLACEHOLDER)

Replace with the approved brand guide. The orchestrator loads this whole file into the
`design_brief` prompt. The image agent appends the chosen "Visual style: …" section (photo by
default) and then the "Image style suffix" to every image prompt. The text of those sections goes
into the prompt word for word, so keep only prompt text under them.

## Voice

- Warm, plain and respectful. Speak to the reader as "คุณ".
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Never use fear to sell. Talk about being ready, not about being sick.
- No superlatives or promises ("best", "guaranteed", "100%").

## Copy structure (single image with CTA)

- Headline: up to 60 characters.
- Body: 1–3 short sentences.
- Benefit lines: 1–3, each backed by an approved product fact.
- CTA: a soft invitation, e.g. "คุยกับที่ปรึกษา".

## Colour and look

- Warm, natural light. One red accent colour in the scene.
- Clean, uncluttered backgrounds with room for copy.

## Image style suffix

all people are Thai (Southeast Asian appearance), modern Thai home or city setting, clean uncluttered background, one subtle red accent, 4:5 vertical framing, absolutely no text or lettering anywhere (plain book covers, blank screens, no signs, no packaging labels), no logos, no watermark

## Visual style: photo

photorealistic lifestyle photography, natural warm light, soft depth of field

## Visual style: cartoon

friendly flat 2D cartoon illustration, clean rounded outlines, soft warm pastel palette, simple shapes, cheerful expressive characters, not photorealistic

## Visual style: 3d

soft 3D rendered illustration, rounded stylised characters, gentle studio lighting, warm colours, not photorealistic
